Smackyard Privacy Policy

Game: Smackyard for Android (package com.smackyard.game)
Made by Boneloom Studio
Effective date: 16 November 2026

English · Español

The short version. Smackyard has no accounts, and we, the people who make it, collect nothing about you. Your fighters and your progress stay on your phone. The game can show ads, and only when you tap a button to watch one. The ads come from Google AdMob, and to show them Google receives your phone's advertising ID, a rough idea of where you are (worked out from your internet address) and some technical information. If you buy something, Google Play takes the payment and a service called RevenueCat checks the purchase so the game can give you what you bought; we never see your card. We do not sell anything about you.

Who we are

Smackyard is a game made by Boneloom Studio, an independent developer in Chiriquí, Panama. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean Boneloom Studio. You can reach us at boneloomstudio@gmail.com.

What we collect ourselves: nothing

We have no servers that receive information from the game, and there is no sign-up or sign-in. We do not know who you are.

  • Your game is saved on your phone. Your fighters, coins, items and progress are kept in the game's private storage on your device. They are never sent to us.
  • Your phone's own backup: if your phone backs up apps to your Google account, Android may include the game's save in that backup. That backup belongs to your Google account, and we cannot see it.
  • Checking the time: so that daily rewards stay fair, the game asks a Google server what time it is. The request carries nothing about you or your game.
  • No access to your phone's private things. The game does not ask for your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files or precise (GPS) location.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Smackyard is free, and ads help pay for it. Ads are always your choice: one plays only when you tap a button to watch it in exchange for a reward in the game. There are no ads that appear on their own.

The ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, to count them and to stop fraud, Google's ad software inside the game collects the following automatically:

WhatExamplesWhy
Device identifiersYour phone's advertising ID (a number you can reset or delete in Android's settings) and an ID for this installation.To show ads, limit how often you see the same ad, and measure ads.
Approximate locationYour country, region or city, worked out from your internet (IP) address. Never your precise location.To show ads that fit your region and language.
Ad interactionsWhich ads were shown, whether an ad was watched to the end, and taps on ads.To give you your reward, measure ads and detect fraud.

This information goes to Google, not to us. Google and its advertising partners may use it to show you ads, including ads based on your interests if you allow that. You can read how Google uses information from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your consent. If you live in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your choice about ads before any are shown, using Google's consent tool (the User Messaging Platform). If you do not consent, you may see ads that are not based on your interests, or no ads at all.

Everywhere, you can turn off personalised ads or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings, then Google, then Ads; on some phones, Settings, then Privacy, then Ads).

Purchases (Google Play and RevenueCat)

Everything in Smackyard can be played for free. The shop has optional purchases: one-time packs, and passes that renew until you cancel them. All of them are bought through Google Play.

  • Payment: Google Play takes the payment. Your card or other payment details go to Google, never to us.
  • RevenueCat: we use RevenueCat, a purchase service, to check purchases with Google Play and to tell the game what you own. RevenueCat receives your purchase history in this game (what was bought, when it was bought or renewed, the price and currency, the store country, and Google's purchase token), an anonymous ID it creates for this installation of the game, and basic device and app information (Android version, app version). It does not receive your name or your email address.
  • Why: to give you what you bought, to restore it on a new phone, and to know how many purchases are made. It is not used for advertising.
  • Cancelling a pass: you can cancel at any time in Google Play, under Payments and subscriptions.

If you write to us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to answer you and to improve the game.

What we do not do

  • No accounts. There is no sign-up or sign-in, and the game never asks for your name, email address or phone number.
  • No analytics of our own. We do not track what you do in the game.
  • No selling. We do not sell your information. The only companies that receive anything are the ones named on this page (Google and RevenueCat), for the reasons described here.

Security

Everything sent to Google and RevenueCat is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Your save is kept in the game's private storage, which other apps on your phone cannot read.

How long it is kept

We keep nothing ourselves, apart from emails you send us. Google keeps advertising information according to its own privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy). Purchase records are kept by Google and RevenueCat for as long as a purchase needs them and as long as tax and accounting rules require (RevenueCat's policy: revenuecat.com/privacy). Your save stays on your phone until you clear the game's storage or uninstall the game.

Your choices

  • Not watching ads. Ads only play when you tap to watch one. If you never tap, you never see one.
  • Ad personalisation and your advertising ID: turn personalised ads off, or reset or delete your advertising ID, in your phone's settings (see the Ads section above).
  • Uninstalling Smackyard stops all collection and removes the game's data from your phone, including your save.
  • Questions or requests about your information: email us at the address above and we will help. Because there are no accounts, we do not know who you are unless you tell us. For information held by Google or RevenueCat, we will point you to the right place.

Children

Smackyard is meant for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child under 13 has sent us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If Smackyard starts collecting anything new, we will update this page and change the effective date above before that version of the game is released.
