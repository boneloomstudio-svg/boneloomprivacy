Boneloom Privacy Policy

App: Boneloom for Android (package com.boneloom.app)
Effective date: 23 September 2026

The short version. Your drawings and projects stay on your phone. Boneloom has no accounts and no ads, and does not use an advertising ID. To find and fix crashes and to see which parts of the app are used, the app automatically sends us technical information through Google's Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Analytics. We don't sell it or share it with anyone else.

Who we are

Boneloom is a drawing and 2D animation app made by an independent developer in Panama. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean the developer of Boneloom. You can reach us at boneloomstudio@gmail.com.

Your drawings and projects stay on your device

Everything you make in Boneloom, including drawings, layers, frames, skeletons, animations, colours and settings, is stored only on your phone, inside the app's private storage. We never receive it, and it is never uploaded to us.

  • Importing a picture: Boneloom uses Android's photo picker. The app only sees the one image you choose, and that image stays on your device.
  • Exporting: when you save a PNG, GIF, video or project file, you choose where it goes. Where you send it after that is up to you.
  • Moving the light by tilting your phone: the app reads your phone's motion sensor while you use this feature. The readings are used on the spot and never stored or sent anywhere.
  • Your phone's own backup: if your phone backs up apps to your Google account, Android may include Boneloom's files in that backup. That backup belongs to your Google account, and we cannot see it.

What the app collects automatically

Boneloom uses two Google services, Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Analytics. They collect the following automatically while you use the app:

WhatExamplesWhy
Crash logsWhat the app was doing when it crashed, and the error.To find and fix crashes.
DiagnosticsPhone model, Android version, app version, memory and performance information, language and country setting.To understand crashes and problems on different phones.
App interactionsWhich screens are opened, how long a session lasts, and basic events such as the app being opened or updated.To learn which parts of the app are used, so we can improve them.
Device identifiersA random ID that Firebase creates for this installation of the app.To tell one installation's reports apart from another's.

This information does not include your name, email address, contacts, precise location, photos, or anything you draw. It is not used to identify you personally.

What we do not do

  • No accounts. There is no sign-up or sign-in.
  • No ads. Boneloom shows no advertising.
  • No advertising ID. The app does not collect or use your device's advertising ID, and ad personalisation is turned off.
  • No selling or sharing. We do not sell your information or share it with third parties. Google runs Crashlytics and Analytics for us as a service provider and handles this information on our behalf.

Security

Everything sent to Firebase is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Your drawings and projects are kept in the app's private storage, which other apps on your phone cannot read.

How long it is kept

Crash reports are kept by Firebase Crashlytics for 90 days. Analytics information is kept according to Firebase Analytics' retention setting and is then deleted automatically. Your drawings stay on your phone until you delete them or uninstall the app.

Your choices

  • Uninstalling Boneloom stops all collection and removes the app's data from your phone, including your drawings. Export anything you want to keep first.
  • Clearing the app's storage in Android's settings resets the app's random ID, which also removes your drawings.
  • Questions or requests about your information: email us at the address above and we'll help. Because there are no accounts, we don't know who you are unless you tell us.

Children

Boneloom is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app collects the same technical information from everyone, as described above. If you believe a child has contacted us with personal information, email us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If Boneloom starts collecting anything new, for example when in-app purchases are added, we will update this page and change the effective date above before that version of the app is released.
